## Known Limitations (Documented in DESIGN.md)

1. **Rate Table Extraction Not Implemented**
   - Impact: Cannot detect overcharge findings
   - Reason: Complex PDF table parsing requires specialized library (tabula-py/pdfplumber)
   - Estimated time needed: 4-6 hours for robust implementation

2. **Rebate Tier Extraction Incomplete**
   - Impact: Limited cashback detection capability
   - Reason: Regex pattern exists but not extracting from all contract formats
   - Estimated time needed: 2-3 hours

3. **Payment Ledger Integration Needs Enhancement**
   - Impact: Late fee risk calculation limited
   - Reason: Payment date extraction patterns need expansion
   - Estimated time needed: 1-2 hours
